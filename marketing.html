HOW IT
WORKS.

01
🗺️
Find a Grime Zone
Open the map. See where trash has been spotted by the community. Pick your target — a park, a beach, a street corner. Every zone has a threat level and an Elemonster boss.
02
🗑️
Clean It Up
Go to the zone. Pick up real trash. The AI doesn't care if it's one bag or twenty — it cares that you showed up and cleaned something that was dirty.
03
📸
Prove It. AI Verifies.
Take a photo of the cleaned area. Claude AI verifies the cleanup is real. No photo fraud. No fake XP. The game's integrity is the whole point.
04
⚡
Earn XP. Spawn Elemonsters.
Verified cleanups earn XP and a chance to spawn an Elemonster — creatures born from the pollution you removed. Collect them. Level them. Raid bosses with your clan.
